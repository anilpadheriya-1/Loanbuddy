Privacy Policy — Loan Reality India

Effective 26 September 2026. Applies to the Loan Reality India website and the Loan Reality India Android app.

Loan Reality India is an educational loan-cost calculator for Indian borrowers. It is not a lender, loan agent or marketplace, and it does not offer, arrange or facilitate loans.

1. Your loan information stays on your device

  • All calculations run on your device. We do not operate a server that receives the loan details you enter.
  • Answers in progress, saved loans, comparisons and your theme and language preferences are stored only on your device (in the browser’s or app’s local storage). We cannot see them.
  • We never put your financial data in page addresses (URLs).
  • There are no accounts or sign-up.

2. Information we never ask for

We never ask for your PAN, Aadhaar, date of birth, address, bank account or card numbers, UPI IDs, passwords or OTPs, and we never check your credit score. Nothing you enter is shared with any lender.

3. Ads in the Android app (Google AdMob)

The website shows no ads. The free Android app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, a Google advertising service, when the phone is online. With no internet, the app works fully and shows no ads.

  • To deliver, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device’s advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The app never sends your loan inputs, saved loans or reports to Google or anyone else. Ad requests contain no information from the calculator — no amounts, rates, lender names, keywords or page content.
  • We do not choose individual ads, and an ad is not a recommendation by us.
  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time under Privacy → “Ad privacy choices” in the app.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Links to other websites

Links to official sources such as the Reserve Bank of India, CBIC or credit bureaus open those websites, which have their own privacy policies. We have no control over them.

5. Deleting your data

Use “Clear my saved data” on the Privacy page to delete everything the website or app has stored on your device. Uninstalling the Android app also deletes its data. Cloud backup of app data is turned off, so it is not copied to other devices.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

7. Security

Because your loan information never leaves your device, there is no copy of it on our side to protect. Protect your device with a screen lock if others use it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer at the email address shown on the app’s Google Play store listing.
