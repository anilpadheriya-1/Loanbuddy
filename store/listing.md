## English (en-IN)

**Short description** (78/80)

> Find your loan's real cost: fees, GST, insurance and EMIs, in ₹, step by step.

**Full description**

> The interest rate is only one part of what a loan costs. Processing fees, GST on fees, insurance, advance EMIs and deductions from the amount you receive can make the real cost noticeably higher.
>
> Loan Reality India rebuilds your loan's cash flows and shows, in plain language:
>
> • Estimated effective annualized cost vs the quoted rate, with a step-by-step explanation of the difference
> • Net amount you actually received vs the loan amount
> • Every charge in ₹: processing fee, GST, insurance, documentation and more
> • EMI check and flat-rate detection
> • Full repayment schedule (table, yearly view and chart)
> • Loan Deal Score (0–100) with every point explained, plus a data-confidence level
> • Ask Your Lender: a checklist of the details to request (KFS, APR, prepayment and foreclosure charges) with a ready-made message in English or Hindi, and the real ROI updating as you fill them in
> • Savings Lab: extra EMIs, lump-sum prepayment, lower EMI vs shorter tenure, rate negotiation and balance-transfer break-even
> • Compare loan offers, save your loans, and read 26 short guides on how loan cost works
> • RBI rules that may apply, with links to the official sources
>
> Works for home, vehicle, personal, education, gold, business and consumer loans.
>
> Private by design
> • No sign-up. No PAN, Aadhaar or bank details. No credit-score check.
> • Your loan numbers stay on your phone; calculations run on the device.
> • Works fully offline.
>
> Free, supported by ads from Google. Ads are shown only when you're online, never on the loan-entry screens, and they never receive your loan numbers.
>
> Loan Reality India is an educational calculator. It is not a lender, loan agent or marketplace, and it does not offer or arrange loans. Results are estimates based on what you enter and may differ from the lender's official APR in the Key Fact Statement (KFS). Always check your KFS and loan agreement.
